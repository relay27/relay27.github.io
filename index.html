01 / FIELD NOTES

Built with a technician’s mindset.

I’m Kostas Economou. An autotech technician, Raspberry Pi tinkerer and hobbyist vibe coder. RELAY/27 is my working concept for a very ordinary problem: a phone ringing while someone is already doing the job.

My approach is mechanical: trace the signal, isolate the failure, make the next action visible. The goal is useful intake and a dependable handoff, with the business still in control of dispatch.

02 / FIELD NOTES
