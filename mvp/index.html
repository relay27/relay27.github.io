01 / FIELD NOTES

Installation is a process.

Kostas is taking the concept into three Toronto pilot companies. The rollout sequence below is the plan for each installation, not a report that every stage is already complete.

A / Configuration

Agree on hours, service area, approved greeting, fee language, retention and primary/backup contacts. Record who owns changes.

B / Bench testing

Run synthetic calls covering accents, interruption, silence, bad addresses, duplicates and disconnected transfers. Do not use customer recordings without an approved basis.

C / Supervised overflow

Start with a restricted call window and a human watching the queue. Retain a direct rollback route to the original phone workflow.

D / Daily review

Review missed handoffs, false urgency, caller corrections and complaints. Expand only when owners accept the evidence.

02 / FIELD NOTES

What the MVP does—and what waits.

In scope for the pilot build

  • Approved greeting and basic intake
  • Callback details confirmed aloud
  • Routine queue vs human escalation
  • Delivery and acknowledgement status
  • Owner review and rollback controls

Deferred until validated

  • Autonomous dispatch or guaranteed arrival
  • Binding estimates and payment collection
  • Diagnosis or repair instructions
  • Unsupervised calendar changes
  • Wide public rollout
03 / FIELD NOTES

Acceptance criteria before expansion.

Proposed gates: every synthetic safety scenario takes the approved route; retries do not create duplicate work orders; every escalation has an acknowledged or visibly failed status; a provider outage triggers the documented fallback.

Track capture completeness, routing agreement with a reviewer, response time, transfer failures and cost per handled call. Establish numerical targets with each company after baseline measurement. No live metrics are claimed here.
